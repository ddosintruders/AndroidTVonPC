## Improvements

### Adding remote control

You will see that our system works like a PC. Not like a native smart television like remote control.  

To fix this, we can add a Bluetooth remote control functionality, the only requirement being, you must have a bluetooth-enabled PC. (Most of modern computers have Bluetooth)  

If not, you can opt to buy a ==[Wi-Fi + Bluetooth M.2 Card](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=m2+wifi+card&crid=2QSW79PXLONM5&sprefix=m2+wifi+card%2Caps%2C433&ref=nb_sb_noss_1)== that is to be installed inside your computer, or a [Bluetooth Dongle](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=bluetooth+dongle&crid=3AEIKXOIBYCZY&sprefix=bluetooth+dongk%2Caps%2C472&ref=nb_sb_noss_2) that is to be inserted to one of the USB Ports.

Next, you can either

- Grab a physical [Bluetooth remote](https://www.amazon.com/s?crid=2HR07N4HG8LW&k=bluetooth%20remote&ref=glow_cls&refresh=1&sprefix=bluetooth%20remote%2Caps%2C619)

or

- Download a [Bluetooth Remote Control](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.atharok.btremote.gplay&hl=en-US) app from your phone (BLE - Bluetooth Low Energy Required on your phone).

Then, you can just pair the phone to the Android system by going into ==Settings >> Remotes & Accessories >> Pair a device==

### A note about local TV Channels

An OEM smart TV has the functionality of utilising signals to serve local channels, but in our case, this feature won't work (your PC can't receive these signals without the required equipment and setup)

## Updates

### Updating your system without loosing data

As time goes by, new Android and Android TV versions will come by, and as for that reason, we have to update the system periodically.

Here is how to update your system:

- Grab the latest copy of [Android TV ISO Image](https://android-tvno-pc.my.canva.site) and mount it.
- Copy the ```system.sfs``` file and paste it into the persistence partition we made (the exFAT partition). This will overwrite the existing ```system.sfs``` file without loosing your apps and games.

!!! note "Future Android TV versions"

    Soon, the site where we download the ISO images may not be updated frequently, so we encourage users to actively hunt for new ISOs from [HmTheBoy154's Android TV on PC Telegram Group.](https://t.me/Android_roms_x86)
